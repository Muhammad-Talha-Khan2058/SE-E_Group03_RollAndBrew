FAQ & House Rules

Everything you need to know before playing at Roll & Brew.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. How much does it cost to play?

It is 500 PKR per person for unlimited access to our library of 500+ board games during your visit.

2. Do we need to know the rules beforehand?

Not at all! Our friendly staff is happy to recommend a game and teach you the rules before you start.

3. Can we bring outside food or drinks?

No outside food or drinks are allowed, as we serve fresh coffee, teas, and snacks right from our cafe counter.

4. Do we need a reservation?

Walk-ins are always welcome! However, Friday and Saturday evenings can get busy, so calling ahead for large groups is recommended.

Our 4 House Rules

  1. Use coasters for all drinks: Keep cups on coasters or side tables so game boards stay dry.
  2. Treat game pieces with care: Please return all cards, dice, and tokens back into the box when finished.
  3. Keep noise friendly: Cheer for your wins, but keep volume comfortable for neighboring tables.
  4. Be kind to everyone: We are a welcoming space for beginners, families, and veteran gamers alike.
