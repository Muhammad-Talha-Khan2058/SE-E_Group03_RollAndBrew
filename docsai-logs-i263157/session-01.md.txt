      info if present.

      X of 10) and Bootstrap progress bar, runs a 20-second
      countdown (startQuestionTimer) with warning styling at
      <= 5s, handles keyboard navigation (Enter, Space, and
      instant feedback, and marks timed-out questions wrong
      before advancing (handleTimeOut).
      • Result Screen: displays final score, percentage,
      score-based board game title (getScoreTitle including
      "Catan Champion" and "Casual Player"), a 10-question
      review list (renderAnswerReview), "Play Again", "Back
      to Games" (games.html), and "Switch Player".
      • Leaderboard & Persistence: stores
      rollAndBrew_playerName, rollAndBrew_bestScore, and the
      top 5 rollAndBrew_leaderboard entries in localStorage
      with try/catch guards and type validation
      (getLeaderboard, getSavedBestScore) so missing or
      corrupted localStorage data falls back safely and shows
      "No scores yet" when empty.
